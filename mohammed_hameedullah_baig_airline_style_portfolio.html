02 / Featured case study

Bring clarity to the
job application journey.

A product discovery concept focused on helping job seekers understand what happens after they apply — and improving trust through clearer communication.

From application uncertainty to a more transparent experience.

Research highlighted a key pain point: applicants often receive little or no visibility into application status. The proposed direction focuses on clearer progress signals and timely updates, supported by measurable pilot experiments.

Product discovery concept
STEP 01

Research the problem

Explore user interviews and survey insights to understand applicant uncertainty, repeated effort, and loss of trust.

STEP 02

Shape the experience

Propose application timelines, outcome updates, clearer job status, recruiter reminders, and ATS integrations.

STEP 03

Define success

Establish success metrics, pilot experiments, risks, and guardrails to evaluate whether the solution helps users.
