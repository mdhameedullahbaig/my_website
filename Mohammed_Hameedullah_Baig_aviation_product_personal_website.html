02 / Selected product work

Product discovery in action.

Hands-on case studies exploring user friction, prioritisation and solution direction across aviation and recruitment experiences.

✈ Aviation · Digital booking

Improving the IndiGo App Booking Experience

Reviewed 30 negative online reviews of IndiGo’s digital experience and identified app startup lag and buffering as a key friction point preventing passengers from reaching flight search.

  • Prioritised the app startup experience as a key problem area.
  • Developed a solution direction focused on simplifying app startup, optimising essential configurations and reducing unnecessary launch activities.
  • Defined success metrics around app launch time, early journey drop-offs and bookings per user.

Recruitment · Digital experience

Improving LinkedIn Job Applications for Job Seekers

Conducted 5 user interviews and surveyed 19 job seekers to understand challenges after submitting job applications.

  • 14 of 19 respondents received no application updates.
  • 8 of 19 reported switching job portals due to a lack of progress visibility.
  • Prioritised application transparency and developed a concept featuring automated outcome updates, application timelines, job-status visibility and potential ATS integrations.
